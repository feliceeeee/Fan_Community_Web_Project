THE LEGEND OF CLASH OF BANG

THE ORIGIN: WHERE THE LEGENDS ARE BORN

Clash of BaNG is a fantasy tactical game born from the fusion of two legendary worlds, the strategic base building of a clash universe and the fast-paced hero combat of a battle arena. In this world, commanders do not just build and defend, they also lead powerful heroes into the heat of battle.

What started as a small fan community in 2018 quickly grew into something bigger. Players from across Indonesia united over their obsession with one thing: the Dark Troops, the most powerful and feared units ever to walk the Clash of BaNG universe.

THE CHRONICLES: OUR GLOBAL CONQUEST

2018 : The Dark Genesis

Founded as a small online forum dedicated to Dark Troops strategy, uniting the first wave of tactical players across Indonesia.

2021 : The Jakarta Command Center

Rapid growth led to the establishment of our first physical regional hub in Jakarta, bringing local commanders face to face.

2023 : Reinforcement Hubs

Expanded with two new regional hubs in Bandung and Sanur, forming the three pillars of the Clash of BaNG community.

2026 : The Global Portal

Launching this website to centralize all guides, troop information, and clan activity — and to carry our legacy to fans worldwide.
